Myoelectric Device

$32,000

Our advanced myoelectric device uses cutting-edge muscle-sensing technology to provide intuitive control of prosthetic limbs. By detecting electrical signals from remaining muscles, it enables natural, thought-controlled movement.

  • High-precision EMG sensors
  • Pattern recognition for multiple movements
  • Self-learning algorithm adaptation
  • Wireless muscle signal transmission
  • Low-latency response system
  • Compatible with various prosthetic types
  • Easy calibration process
  • Mobile app for monitoring and adjustment
